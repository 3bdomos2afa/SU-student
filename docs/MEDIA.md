# 🎬 Media kit — SU+ 1.15.0

[← Back to README](../README.md)

Videos, posters and a PDF showcase of SU+ 1.15.0. Posters use the Thmanyah font, show an Android phone mockup and carry the **@3bdomos2afa** signature. Android only.

## Videos

| Video | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [▶️ Demo.mp4](../media/videos/SU-Plus-1.15.0-Demo.mp4) | The live app inside an Android phone mockup, page by page, with a caption above the phone |
| [▶️ Showcase.mp4](../media/videos/SU-Plus-1.15.0-Showcase.mp4) | All posters as an animated slideshow |

Both are H.264 + AAC, 1080×1920 / 1080×1350, and play on any phone or on WhatsApp.
