[← Back to README](../README.md)

Real SU+ 1.15.0 pages with sample data (ID 123456789), light on the left and dark on the right. SU+ is **Android only**. Videos and posters are in [MEDIA](MEDIA.md).

## Home

### Home · الرئيسية

- Short name with the student ID under it, a red fire streak badge and the language chip.
- A hero card "ناجح في X ساعة" with "من 144 ساعة في البرنامج" under it.
- Services, today's classes and absence.
- The study plan: week strip (Saturday first), "إنجاز النهارده" gauge, and tasks with a round green check and a black "ضيف مهمة" button.

## Timetable and study

### Timetable · جدولي

- Black day chips across the top.
- Pastel cards: lilac for lectures, yellow for sections, lime for the class happening now with a "دلوقتي" badge.
- A time column, room and instructor on every card.

## KMoodle

### KMoodle files · ملفات مودل

- Each course is a coloured folder (lime, lilac, yellow, pink, blue).
- A "studied ✓" circle next to every file: it turns green, the name is struck through and "ذاكرته ✓" appears. The mark is saved on the phone.
- Old copies of a course are labelled with their year and term, e.g. "ترم أول · سنة 2024/25" or "سمر · سنة 2024/25".
- Signs in automatically after the first Google sign-in.
